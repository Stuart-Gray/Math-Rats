Louise's recipe card

The two-step recipe

  1. Look at x. What was done to it? Usually it was multiplied (or divided), then something was added (or subtracted).
  2. Undo the + or − first by doing the opposite to both sides.
  3. Undo the × or ÷ next, again on both sides.
  4. Check it! Put your answer back in. If both sides match, you win the snack.
Negative numbers: some answers are below zero, like the attic thermometer. To type one, type the number, then tap +/− to make it negative (or type a minus sign first). Tap +/− again to make it positive. On a number line, adding moves right and subtracting moves left, even past zero.
Calculator: in step-by-step mode, tap Calculator to check your mental math before you press Check. Try it in your head first!
Opposites: + undoes −, × undoes ÷.  Seesaw rule: whatever you do to one side, do to the other so it stays balanced.
One-step: only one thing was done to x, so one opposite undoes it. x + 5 = 12 → subtract 5. 3x = 18 → divide by 3.
x on both sides: in 5x + 2 = 3x + 10, first subtract 3x from both sides to gather the x's. Then it's a normal two-step: 2x + 2 = 10.
Brackets are different: in 3(x + 2) = 15 the + 2 is stuck inside with x, so divide by 3 first, then subtract 2. With 2(x + 3) − 4 = 10, work from the outside in: add 4, divide by 2, then subtract 3.
